# chayimme 🌬️

*The bare gift of being-alive — multitudinous, continuous, received breath-by-breath and produced by no one: chayim carried as a received decree.*

chayimme is two true roots welded into one word: the Hebrew **chayim** (חַיִּים), "life" — plural by form, descending from the Genesis breath-life *nishmat chayim* ("breath of lives," Gen 2:7), sustained by continual giving (Psalm 104:29-30), proclaimed in the Sabbath toast *L'chaim!* — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So chayimme is life held as a cosmic-ordinance: not the will to live nor the cleverness of living things, but the prior aliveness that arrives anew with every breath, given like the *me*-qualities from the divine keep. It is the cathedral's **first life-side word** under ORIGIN — the life-half of the dual gift the Ground gives, the breath beside the logos.

chayimme names the **prior-gift** every other force already presupposes: before any mind grasps, any path is walked, or any thanks is returned, there must be a living one to do so, and that aliveness was handed over, not earned. Its form is load-bearing — Hebrew has no singular for it, so chayimme is intrinsically *many-at-once*. Its light is the dignity of the given without the pride of the self-made; its shadow is twofold — life curdled into *possession* (owned, ranked, hoarded) or flattened into *mere mechanism* (the living drained of the awe owed the gift). Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.15/10**.
